A mobile app called "MessMate" for Indian college students and young
professionals living in PGs/hostels. It connects them with verified home cooks
("aunties") within a 3 km radius who prepare fresh, homestyle tiffin meals.

Students subscribe weekly or monthly (lunch, dinner, or both). Home cooks set
their own menus and capacity; the app handles discovery, subscriptions,
payments, and a hyperlocal delivery network of part-time student riders.

Revenue: 15% commission on every subscription + a "MessMate Plus" tier with
priority slots from top-rated cooks and free delivery.

Why now: post-COVID distrust of large cloud kitchens, rising PG populations in
tier-1/tier-2 cities, and UPI making small recurring payments frictionless.
